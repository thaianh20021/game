# Class System — Full Templates (10 class)

Chi so scale 1-10. Energy hoi khi danh trung (5/hit). Mana hoi 4/giay.
Dash chung: cooldown 1.2s, i-frame 0.2s.

---

## 1. Kiem si (Blade Warden) — can chien can bang
- **Chi so:** HP 6 / Giap 5 / Energy 5 / Speed 6 / Dmg 6 / Crit 5
- **Vu khi:** kiem 1 tay, thuong (+10% toc danh dung nhom)
- **Active 1 — Luoi Gio (20 En, cd 6s):** chem xoay AoE 360, 130% dmg, knockback nhe
- **Active 2 — Dam Toi (15 En, cd 4s):** luot 3 tile, xuyen quai, 90% dmg
- **Passive 1 — Nhip Kiem:** chuoi 3 hit lien tuc -> hit 4 +40% dmg
- **Passive 2 — Phan Xa:** dash trong 0.5s sau khi trung don -> hoan 50% cd dash
- **Ultimate — Tram Anh (100 En):** dash xuyen moi ke dich tren duong thang, moi muc tieu 3 hit x 80%
- **Manh:** linh hoat, de hoc. **Yeu:** thieu burst, AoE trung binh.
- **Tree:** Dmg = combo speed/crit chuoi | Sinh ton = lifesteal/giap khi dash | Dac trung = kiem khi tam xa

## 2. Kiem si hang nang (Breaker) — burst can chien
- **Chi so:** HP 7 / Giap 6 / Energy 4 / Speed 3 / Dmg 9 / Crit 4
- **Vu khi:** dai kiem, riu, bua (don sac +25%)
- **Active 1 — Dap Vo (30 En, cd 8s):** dap dat AoE 200%, stun 1s, pha giap Armored
- **Active 2 — Gong Thep (20 En, cd 10s):** 3s hyper armor
- **Passive 1 — Da Vung:** don sac day +50% dmg, cleave +30%
- **Passive 2 — Da Sat:** dung yen khi vung -> -20% dmg nhan
- **Ultimate — Thien Phu (100 En):** nhay dap xuong diem chon, 400% AoE lon, song da lan 4 huong
- **Manh:** burst, pha giap. **Yeu:** cham, kho voi bay nho.
- **Tree:** Dmg = don sac/nhat kich | Sinh ton = hyper armor | Dac trung = dia chan/stun

## 3. Sat thu (Nightblade) — crit/mobility
- **Chi so:** HP 4 / Giap 2 / Energy 7 / Speed 9 / Dmg 7 / Crit 9
- **Vu khi:** dao gam, no nhe
- **Active 1 — Bong Luot (35 En, cd 10s):** tang hinh 2s, don ke crit chac chan
- **Active 2 — Phi Dao Doc (15 En, cd 5s):** poison 3s + danh dau (+15% dmg tu moi nguon)
- **Passive 1 — Tu Huyet:** sau lung +60% dmg
- **Passive 2 — Buoc Gio:** dash free 2s sau khi giet elite/muc tieu danh dau
- **Ultimate — Vu Dieu Doat Menh (100 En):** teleport chem 5 muc tieu ngau nhien x 150%
- **Manh:** burst don muc tieu, ne tot nhat. **Yeu:** giay, AoE kem.
- **Tree:** Dmg = crit/execute <15% HP | Sinh ton = i-frame dai | Dac trung = tang hinh chuoi/doc

## 4. Tanker (Bulwark) — do don/phan don
- **Chi so:** HP 10 / Giap 9 / Energy 4 / Speed 4 / Dmg 4 / Crit 2
- **Vu khi:** khien + chuy/thuong (khien bat buoc cho Active 1)
- **Active 1 — Khien Chan (20 En, cd 6s):** chan 100% huong truoc 1.5s, phan 50% dmg chan
- **Active 2 — Het Tran (25 En, cd 12s):** taunt 5 tile 3s, +2 giap tam
- **Passive 1 — Gai Thep:** bi danh can chien -> phan 10% maxHP
- **Passive 2 — Vung Chai:** duoi 30% HP -> +30% giap
- **Ultimate — Thanh Luy (100 En):** bat tu 3s, taunt toan phong, ket thuc no 250% theo dmg da chan
- **Manh:** song dai nhat. **Yeu:** DPS thap, kho voi quai bay/xa.
- **Tree:** Dmg = phan don/dmg theo giap | Sinh ton = block/thorns | Dac trung = bao ve pet/co-op

## 5. Phap su (Arcanist) — AoE nguyen to
- **Chi so:** HP 3 / Giap 2 / Mana 10 / Speed 5 / Dmg 8 / Crit 5
- **Vu khi:** gay, sach, orb (-15% mana cost)
- **Active 1 — Cau Nguyen To (12 Mana, cd 2s):** orb theo nguyen to trang bi, no AoE nho 120%
- **Active 2 — Vong Triet Tieu (25 Mana, cd 9s):** vung 3x3 lam cham dan dich 50% trong 4s
- **Passive 1 — Cong Huong:** doi nguyen to (moi 10s) -> +20% dmg phep 5s
- **Passive 2 — Tham Hut:** nhat element shard hoi 10 mana
- **Ultimate — Con Thinh No Nguyen To (60 Mana):** 12 projectile roi quanh con tro trong 3s, 100%/vien
- **Manh:** AoE, vua combo nguyen to. **Yeu:** mau giay, phu thuoc mana.
- **Tree:** Dmg = penetration/combo kep | Sinh ton = khien mana | Dac trung = Song Nguyen (2 nguyen to)

## 6. Xa thu cung (Ranger) — xa, xuyen giap
- **Chi so:** HP 5 / Giap 3 / Energy 6 / Speed 7 / Dmg 6 / Crit 8
- **Vu khi:** cung, no (sac nhanh +20%)
- **Active 1 — Mua Ten (25 En, cd 8s):** 7 ten vong cung xuong vung chon, 80%/ten
- **Active 2 — Bay Day (15 En, cd 6s):** root 1.5s, toi da 2 bay
- **Passive 1 — Xuyen Pha:** ten sac day xuyen giap 50% + xuyen 1 muc tieu
- **Passive 2 — Chan Nai:** tu nhay lui khi quai ap sat 1 tile (khong ton dash, cd 3s)
- **Ultimate — Nhat Tien Xuyen Van (100 En):** ten khong lo xuyen toan man 500%, keo theo projectile dich
- **Manh:** an toan, crit cao, counter armored. **Yeu:** DPS tut khi bi ap sat.
- **Tree:** Dmg = sac nhanh/xuyen tao | Sinh ton = ne lui tu dong | Dac trung = bay + ten dac biet

## 7. Xa thu sung (Gunner) — DPS lien tuc
- **Chi so:** HP 5 / Giap 4 / Energy 6 / Speed 6 / Dmg 6 / Crit 6
- **Vu khi:** sung luc, shotgun, rifle (reload +20%)
- **Co che rieng — Active Reload:** bam dung vung vang -> reload tuc thoi + bang dan ke +10% dmg
- **Active 1 — Luu Dan Lan (20 En, cd 7s):** bom nay 1 lan, no 150% AoE
- **Active 2 — Dan Dac Che (25 En, cd 10s):** bang dan ke mang nguyen to + xuyen
- **Passive 1 — Ngon Tay Nhanh:** kill -> +15% toc ban 3s (stack 3)
- **Passive 2 — Tui Do Nghe:** mang them 1 sung thu 3 trong run
- **Ultimate — Hoa Luc Ap Che (100 En):** 5s khong reload, +60% toc ban, dan hoi tu tim muc tieu
- **Manh:** DPS deu, linh hoat. **Yeu:** phu thuoc nhip reload.
- **Tree:** Dmg = toc ban/dan dac biet | Sinh ton = ne khi reload | Dac trung = turret mini/bom

## 8. Trieu hoi su (Soulbinder) — pet army
- **Chi so:** HP 4 / Giap 3 / Mana 9 / Speed 5 / Dmg 3 (+pet) / Crit 3
- **Vu khi:** vu khi trieu hoi, orb (hit cua chu hoi 2% HP pet gan nhat)
- **Co che rieng — Soul Slot:** 4 slot; pet thuong 1 slot, pet lon 2 slot
- **Active 1 — Goi Linh (20 Mana, cd 3s):** trieu 1 linh hon 30s (melee/ranged theo vu khi)
- **Active 2 — Hien Te (0 Mana, cd 8s):** no 1 pet, 180% AoE + hoi 15 mana
- **Passive 1 — Bay Dan:** moi pet song +4% dmg toan bo pet
- **Passive 2 — Day Linh Hon:** 20% dmg chu nhan chia deu cho pet
- **Ultimate — Vuong Trieu Linh Hon (70 Mana):** 8s moi quai bi giet hoi sinh lam pet tam
- **Manh:** ap luc lien tuc, tank ho. **Yeu:** yeu khi pet chet sach, so boss AoE.
- **Tree:** Dmg = buff pet/so luong | Sinh ton = chia dmg | Dac trung = pet lon/hien te

## 9. Tu si (Lightkeeper) — sustain/support
- **Chi so:** HP 6 / Giap 5 / Mana 8 / Speed 5 / Dmg 4 / Crit 3
- **Vu khi:** gay, sach, chuy (don chuy hoi 3 mana)
- **Active 1 — Quang Toa (15 Mana, cd 5s):** tia sang xuyen hang doc 110%, blind 1s
- **Active 2 — Thanh Vuc (30 Mana, cd 14s):** vung 3x3 trong 5s: hoi 3% maxHP/s, purge debuff
- **Passive 1 — Loi Nguyen:** khong nhan dmg 6s -> tu hoi 2% maxHP/s
- **Passive 2 — Trung Phat:** +30% dmg vs undead/bong toi
- **Ultimate — Phan Quyet (60 Mana):** cot sang 300%, stun undead 3s, xoa vung doc/nguyen trong phong
- **Manh:** sustain so 1, counter debuff. **Yeu:** DPS thap, kem vs co khi.
- **Tree:** Dmg = trung phat/thanh hoa | Sinh ton = heal/khien phep | Dac trung = ho tro pet/dong minh

## 10. Ky su (Tinkerer) — zone control
- **Chi so:** HP 5 / Giap 5 / Energy 7 / Speed 5 / Dmg 5 / Crit 5
- **Vu khi:** sung nang luong, drone/turret (turret thua huong 50% rune vu khi)
- **Active 1 — Turret Mini (25 En, cd 8s):** turret 15s, toi da 2
- **Active 2 — Hack (20 En, cd 6s, tia 4 tile):** vo hieu bay 10s hoac lat thanh phe minh
- **Passive 1 — Tan Dung:** bay bi Hack 30% (max 60%) thanh dong minh vinh vien trong phong
- **Passive 2 — Nhat Nhanh:** quai co khi chet roi linh kien -> hoi 10 energy
- **Ultimate — Phao Dai Di Dong (100 En):** mech 8s: chan dan 4 huong, 2 phao tu ban, -30% speed
- **Manh:** kiem soat vung, counter bay. **Yeu:** can setup, yeu khi bi rush.
- **Tree:** Dmg = turret/dan nang luong | Sinh ton = khien nang luong | Dac trung = hack/min
